# Coding Trainer AI 🧠🤖🎓

> An AI-powered learning studio, syntax drill trainer, flashcard engine, and UK Master's Exam Prep Assistant tailored specifically for **bridging non-CS backgrounds (History & International Studies) into an MSc in AI & Robotics**.

---

## 🎯 Mission & Educational Philosophy

Transitioning from a **History & International Studies** background into a technical **MSc in AI and Robotics** requires two key pillars:

1. **Intuitive Non-CS Bridge:** Abstract computer science, vector space mathematics, and robotics concepts are explained through clear analogies before introducing formal mathematical and programmatic rigor.
2. **UK Master's Marking System Alignment:** Assessments, quizzes, and exam simulations align directly with the **UK Higher Education Marking System**:
   * 🏆 **Distinction (70%+):** Exceptional technical accuracy, mathematical rigor, and critical evaluation.
   * 📜 **Merit (60% - 69%):** Solid implementation, clear logic, good conceptual understanding.
   * ✅ **Pass (50% - 59%):** Minimum threshold required to unlock the next progressive module.
   * ❌ **Fail (< 50%):** Requires retake with dynamic quiz variations.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **🌉 Non-CS Bridge & Intuitive Analogies:** Translates complex CS & robotics topics (e.g. pointers, state machines, graphs, matrices) into clear, non-intimidating concepts.
* **🎴 Flashcards & Spaced Repetition (SRS):** Built-in SuperMemo SM-2 algorithm for daily study decks covering syntax, definitions, formulas, and UK exam essay terms.
* **🎯 Quizzes with Gated Pass Marks:** Mandatory pass threshold (50% Pass / 70% Distinction) to unlock subsequent modules.
* **⚡ Anti-Copilot Syntax Drills:** Practice writing raw code without AI autocompletion to rebuild real muscle memory.
* **📚 Doc-Driven Curriculum (RAG):** Upload official documentation (Python, Swift, C++, ROS 2, PyTorch, OpenCV, CUDA) to auto-generate customized 5-tier learning levels.
* **🧩 DSA Pattern Recognition Track:** Pattern-based problem solving (Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Graph Traversals, Dynamic Programming) with step-by-step whiteboard mode.
* **📝 UK Master's Exam Simulator:** 2-hour timed exams with authentic UK mark schemes and specific feedback on how to upgrade your score from Merit to Distinction.
